Gebaren v2 · step 2: design

Three design directions

All three are working mockups: the video loops, the buttons work and the search box suggests words (type hu, use the arrow keys, press Enter). They share the same content and accessibility features; they differ in layout, type, colour and feel. Pick one, or a mix.

Show screen:
A

Stilte (Quiet)

One calm, centred column. The video sits in a soft frame and everything around it stays quiet.

Type: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next Colour:

What makes it different

  • The same single column on every screen, so nothing moves around between phone and laptop.
  • Atkinson Hyperlegible Next was designed for readers with low vision.
  • Word, variants and Bewaar share one row on a laptop, so the video gets more height.

Watch out: the quietest look, and also the least distinctive. On a 1280×860 laptop the video takes about 60% of the height; bigger screens get more, and full screen is one click.

B

Signaal (Signal)

Bold signage. A yellow search band and thick lines, with the word huge on the left and the video big on the right.

Type: Lexend Colour:

What makes it different

  • Two columns on a big screen. Word, variants and meaning sit on the left, and the video gets the most room of the three.
  • Variants are picture tiles, so you see the difference before you choose.
  • Speed buttons show both the word and the number (Normaal 1×). Lexend is a wide face made for reading fluency.

Watch out: the strongest identity, and also the loudest. In the dark theme the yellow stays as an accent only, because a large yellow band would glare.

C

Zakboek (Pocket)

A friendly pocket app. Round buttons with the label under the icon, a tab bar on the phone, and your lists in a side rail on a big screen.

Type: Andika Colour:

What makes it different

  • Inzoomen crops the empty studio space beside the signer. On a phone held upright the signer is a third bigger (4:3 instead of 16:9). It starts switched on there.
  • The tab bar keeps Zoeken, Recent, Bewaard and Uitleg one tap away. On a laptop those sit in a side rail.
  • Andika was made for people who are learning to read, which fits readers for whom Dutch is a second language.

Watch out: Inzoomen could cut off a sign that uses the full arm span, so it stays a switch. The fixed tab bar is turned off on short screens and at high zoom, so it never hides content.

The same in all three

  • Big controls (44 px or more), each with an icon and a word. On/off states show a check mark, never only a colour.
  • Autoplay, muted and looping, with a big Pauze button. With "reduce motion" on, the video waits for Afspelen.
  • Speed (Normaal, Langzaam, Heel langzaam), Spiegel, Opnieuw and full screen. In full screen our own controls stay visible.
  • Keyboard shortcuts are listed on the page and can be switched off. WCAG 2.1.4 requires this for single-key shortcuts.
  • In dark mode the white studio video is dimmed a little (88%) to cut glare.
  • A search box that suggests words as you type and works fully by keyboard, with status messages for screen readers.
  • Not found: a likely word (huisdeur), the parts of a compound word (huis + dier, a new idea that suits Dutch), tips, and a link to the Gebarencentrum.
  • No cookies, no ads, no pop-ups. Recent searches and Bewaard stay on the device.

How to choose

Reply with a letter, or describe a mix, for example "B's layout with A's colours" or "A, plus Inzoomen from C". Open a live mockup to try it: the dashed Mockup button in its corner switches screens and themes.
